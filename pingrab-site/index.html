    Frequently asked questions

    Short answers to the things people ask before their first download.

    How do I download a Pinterest video?

    Open the pin on Pinterest, tap the three dots and choose Copy link. Paste it into PinGrab and press Get video, then Download MP4. On iPhone the file is saved in the Files app; on Android and desktop it goes to the Downloads folder.

    Is PinGrab free?

    Yes. There is no account, no app install and no email address. The site is supported by ads, which is why you may see an ad block above and below the tool.

    Will the video have a watermark?

    No. PinGrab returns the MP4 that Pinterest itself serves. We do not re-encode it, so there is no extra logo or watermark added by us.

    What quality can I get?

    Most pins are available as a progressive MP4 in 720p with the original audio. The maximum resolution depends on what the creator uploaded. If a pin is only published as an adaptive HLS stream, PinGrab gives you the best direct MP4 that exists.

    Does it work on iPhone and Android?

    Yes. Use Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android. If you opened the site inside Pinterest's in-app browser, tap the menu and choose Open in browser first - in-app browsers often block file downloads.

    Can I download private or deleted pins?

    No. PinGrab can only read public pins. Private boards, deleted pins and pins that Pinterest restricts by region will show an error message.

    Why does PinGrab say this is an image pin?

    Not every pin is a video. When the pin is a photo, PinGrab offers the full-size JPG instead of an MP4, so you can still save what you found.

    Is downloading Pinterest videos legal?

    Download only videos you own or have permission to use, and keep them for personal use. Re-uploading someone else's video can infringe copyright and break Pinterest's terms. PinGrab is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Pinterest, Inc.
